Snug Support

Thank you for playing Snug.

How to play

Arrange the dogs so that no two of them share a row or a column.

  • Flick a dog and it slides until something stops it — the edge of the board, a rock, or another dog
  • You cannot move one square at a time. All you choose is what to stop against
  • Rows and columns holding two dogs are shaded. Clear every shade and the puzzle is solved
  • Five puzzles make one stage

"How to play" inside the app shows all of this in pictures,

and the four opening slides can be watched again from there.

Frequently asked questions

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it.

Restoring your device from an iCloud or computer backup brings it back.

I changed phones. Can I carry on?

Yes, if you restored the new device from a backup.

There is no syncing between devices.

Can I get stuck?

No. Every board is solved by the app before it is dealt to you.

There is no timer, no move limit, and no limit on undo.

Can I skip a hard puzzle?

Yes — "Skip" on the play screen.

A skipped puzzle counts towards neither your moves nor the par.

In exchange, that stage is left out of your best.

How do I collect more stamps?

One arrives for every day you play, and one more at each streak and stage milestone.

The order is shuffled, but there are no duplicates and no blanks.

Nothing is decided by chance, so two people who play the same amount collect the same amount.

About the ads

A full-screen ad may appear between stages.

Nobody who states they are under 13 is shown ads.

App Tracking Transparency permission is never requested, and ads are not personalised.

There is no sound

Please check your device's silent switch, and the sound setting inside the app.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Dogs can be selected and moved by direction through VoiceOver.
